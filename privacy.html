Privacy Policy

VINYL DECK · Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VINYL DECK, a macOS music player developed by Carlos Dafonte Fernandez, interacts with information when you use the app and its support website.

1. Music files and local library

VINYL DECK is designed to play and organize music that you choose to access on your Mac. When you select local music files or folders, the app needs access to those files to provide playback and library features. Music files and their metadata are processed on your device as part of those local features. This does not require you to create an online account.

2. Online radio, television, and external services

VINYL DECK includes internet-based features, including radio and television streams and online channel information. Using these features involves requests to external catalog, stream, and related service providers. Such providers may receive standard connection details, including your public IP address, request times, and technical information about your connection or playback software, and handle that data under their own privacy policies.

Some features may use your public IP address to estimate your country for relevant channels or to check whether a stream is accessible. This is an approximate, network-based country estimate, not a request for your device's precise location. Content availability may depend on your region and the service provider.

3. Information you choose to provide

You do not need to register an account to use the local music player. If you voluntarily contact the developer through GitHub support requests, the information you submit is processed by GitHub according to GitHub's own policies. GitHub Issues are public; do not include passwords, private music files, personal details, or other sensitive information in a public support request.

4. Support website and hosting

The VINYL DECK support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting the website may involve the processing of standard technical access information by GitHub, as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

5. Third-party content and links

Radio stations, television streams, channel listings, and linked external websites are operated by independent third parties. Their availability, data handling, and privacy practices are outside the developer's direct control. Please review the policies of those services if you choose to use them.

6. Data protection and your choices

You control which local files and folders you make available to the app and can stop using online streaming features at any time. macOS permissions can be reviewed in System Settings. For questions about information handling or privacy rights, please use the VINYL DECK support page to request guidance without posting private information publicly.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as VINYL DECK develops or its connected services change. The latest version and its update date will be available on this page.
