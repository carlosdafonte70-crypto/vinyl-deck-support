Help & troubleshooting

Frequently asked questions

My music files are not showing. What should I do?

Check that the files are in the selected folder and are in a format the app supports. If the files are on an external drive, confirm that the drive is connected and try choosing the folder again.

I can't hear any sound. How can I fix it?

Check the app volume and the Mac's selected output device in System Settings → Sound. Try playing a different file and check whether another app can play audio.

Album artwork is missing.

Some files do not contain embedded cover artwork. Check the metadata of the affected files and confirm that any separate artwork files are available.

Can I listen to internet radio without an internet connection?

No. Internet radio streams require a working network connection. Music stored locally on your Mac can be used without an active stream.

How do I change the player appearance?

Look for the design or appearance selection control in the app. Available themes and panel options may differ between versions.

How do I report a bug?

Use the support request link below. Include your app version, macOS version, what happened, and how to reproduce the problem. Please do not include private information.
